#### Featured projects

**[Career Matrix](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar/CareerMatrix)** — A full-stack AI platform that scores resume-to-role skill gaps and turns the result into a learning path. Multi-agent RAG where CrewAI agents parse resumes and job descriptions, retrieve from a pgvector store and score gaps, cutting manual research 70%, inside a Canvas-style LMS with courses, quizzes and progress tracking.
`Python` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `pgvector` `LangChain` `CrewAI` `Docker`

**[InventiFlow](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar/Inventiflow)** — A multi-tenant supply chain platform where one order moves stock, production and purchasing on a single schema. Four role dashboards with row-level security via Supabase Auth, plus SKU-level demand forecasting (XGBoost, Prophet) and Monte Carlo what-if simulation that improved simulated demand-supply alignment 35%.
`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase` `Python` `XGBoost` `Prophet` `Databricks`

**[MumBus](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar/mumbus-transit)** — A real-time transit system that detects congestion as it forms and plans trips around it, in any city. Live vehicle-location events stream through Kafka into PostGIS, DBSCAN clusters hotspots, and Mapbox updates push to the client over WebSockets; stops seed from OpenStreetMap so a new city is one config change.
`Python` `FastAPI` `Kafka` `Spark Structured Streaming` `scikit-learn` `PostGIS` `React Native` `Mapbox`

**[SummarEase](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar/SummarEase)** — A document intelligence workspace that keeps the source material, not just the summary. A fine-tuned BERT model reaches 0.42 ROUGE-L with 30% faster batched inference, and nine modules implement published algorithms directly — Smith-Waterman with affine gaps, STFT fingerprinting, CRDTs, SHA-256 hash chains with Merkle proofs — carrying 523 passing checks and no dependency on an AI service. Awarded 1st Prize, IETE-RAIT Winternship.
`Python` `Flask` `SQLAlchemy` `NumPy` `PyTorch` `BERT`

**[Transcript Hub](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar/Highschool-Transcript-Depot)** — An admissions records platform that replaced manual transcript re-keying, built at CSULB Institutional Research & Analytics. Layout-aware Document AI extraction into a typed record validated before any write, OTP-verified requests, and a three-role review workflow across student, school and reviewer.
`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React` `TypeScript` `Google Document AI` `Terraform` `GCP`

**[Wage Gap Insights](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar/Gender-Wage-Gap-Analysis)** — An econometric study of the gender wage gap over a 24-year labour panel, built at the LMU Datathon. Job- and industry-fixed-effects models over ~170,000 NLSY97 job observations isolate a 10.2% female wage penalty (p < 0.001), with California's 2018 salary history ban simulated as a counterfactual.
`Python` `Pandas` `statsmodels` `Chart.js`

**[SignSpeak](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar)** — A computer vision system that translates static sign language gestures into real-time text, built for hearing and speech impaired users. Low-latency webcam capture in OpenCV, 21 hand landmarks per frame from MediaPipe Hands, and a random forest classifier over landmark data recognising the static alphabet A to Z, with predictions rendered live over the camera feed.
`Python` `OpenCV` `MediaPipe` `scikit-learn` `NumPy` `Tkinter`

**[Fake News Detector](https://github.com/Ojas-suratkar/fake-news-detector)** — An end-to-end NLP pipeline that classifies news articles as fake or real: preprocessing and feature extraction, training and evaluation against held-out data, shipped as both a CLI predictor and a Streamlit app.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `NLTK` `Streamlit`
